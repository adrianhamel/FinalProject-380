VII. LEGACY AND MODERNIZATION

By the 2010s, Battle.net evolved into the Blizzard Launcher, adding patch tools, voice chat, cross game social systems, and digital distribution of their titles. Although it became more controlled, the core ideas from 1996 continued to influence design decisions. Modern platforms such as Discord, Steam, Xbox Live, and PSN continue to use systems Battle.net pioneered with streaming services like Twitch or YouTube were built in to provide quick access streaming. The centralized accounts, social environments and groups, and integrated multiplayer features remain a standard in gaming. Battle.net proved that online games were not just about the gameplay but also about belonging to a community.
