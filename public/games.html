BATTLE.NET GAMES

DIABLO

Released in 1996, Diablo was the first game to launch with Battle.net support. This dark fantasy action RPG introduced millions of players to online multiplayer gaming through Battle.net's revolutionary platform. Players could join together to fight demons, trade items, and explore the depths of the Tristram Cathedral in real-time multiplayer sessions.

STARCRAFT

StarCraft, released in 1998, became one of the most popular games on Battle.net. Its competitive multiplayer scene, especially in South Korea, helped establish esports as a global phenomenon. The game's map editor and custom map sharing through Battle.net chatrooms gave birth to entirely new gaming genres and gameplay styles.

WARCRAFT II

Warcraft II: Battle.net Edition brought the classic real-time strategy game to Battle.net in 1998. This expansion of Battle.net's game library further solidified the platform's dominance in online gaming. The game's strategic multiplayer battles and clan systems helped shape the competitive gaming landscape.
